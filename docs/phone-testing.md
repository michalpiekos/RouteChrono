# Recording and phone checks

1. Select the **Record** tab, grant precise location and notifications, and press **Start** at your starting point. Wait for GPS before travelling. Press **Stop** at your destination. The first and last accepted GPS positions become a saved pair when they are over 150 m apart and were captured within 15 seconds of the button presses.
2. Alternatively, open **Pairs → Add pair on map**, tap the start and end points, optionally name the pair, and save. To measure from one start to several destinations, press **Add end** and tap each further end (up to five); **Remove last end** drops the last one, and a single end is a simple pair. All points must be over 150 m apart. Use **My location** to center the map and show a blue dot with a white ring at the retrieved position. Tap the dot to see the fix time; press **My location** again to refresh it. The dot survives redraws and rotation and represents the last located position, not continuous tracking. Tap elsewhere on the map to set an endpoint. Save as many pairs as needed. Rename or remove pairs here; removal retains existing journey results.
3. Saving a pair enables **Automatic recording**. Tap the large **Automatic recording · ON/OFF** button on Record to toggle it. The dark app shows a green glow while monitoring and a stronger glow during a journey; the status text distinguishes monitoring, recording, waiting for GPS and paused states. An enabled toggle with stopped tracking does not show a live glow. Leave it enabled and travel from either endpoint to the other. All saved pairs are monitored together, and different physical routes are recorded independently. The notification identifies monitoring or recording; its **Exit** action stops all tracking and closes the app.
4. Open **Results**, choose a pair, and select a journey. Outward and return trips have separate summaries. A route with several ends groups trips as Start → End N and End N → Start for each end; a start trip that reached no end is listed under Start → no end reached. Each journey shows its recorded path, elapsed time, estimated distance and average speed. Interrupted recordings stay visible but do not affect completed-trip summaries. To remove one finished recording, open its details, tap **Delete recording**, then confirm **Delete**. This permanently removes that recording and its GPS path, updates the summaries, and keeps saved endpoint pairs and other recordings. **Cancel** keeps everything. Stop an active recording before deleting it.
5. Press **Exit** at the top of any tab or in the expanded notification when finished. It disables automatic monitoring, preserves unfinished recordings as interrupted, stops GPS and the background service, removes the notification, and closes the app. Reopening stays paused until you press Start or enable Automatic recording. Android may retain an idle cached process.

During teaching, the map zooms out one level (half the visual scale) when the current position approaches the edge, keeping the starting point and current position visible. For a single active saved pair, both saved endpoints stay visible along with the live route. When multiple pairs are recording simultaneously, the map follows their recorded starts and current positions without locking onto one pair.

Automatic recording requires seeing the phone within 50 m of an endpoint before departure. Leaving beyond 75 m starts the trip using the last inside sample; reaching within 50 m of the opposite endpoint finishes it. With several ends, a trip from the start finishes at the first end reached, and a trip from any end finishes only at the start. Reaching another end instead closes that trip as interrupted (no cooldown), and leaving that end starts a new trip to the start. If the phone gets more than 1.5 times the reference distance away from the trip's origin in a straight line, the trip is abandoned: it and its GPS path are deleted, a silent "Trip abandoned" notification names the pair, and Record shows the same message. The reference is the start-to-end distance (the farthest end for a route with several ends) for trips from the start, and the end-to-start distance for trips from an end. Only that pair's trip is abandoned; a new one starts after the phone is seen at one of its points again. Start and Stop teaching always saves a simple pair; a taught trip between the start and one end of an existing multi-end route is filed under that pair. After a trip ends at either endpoint, that endpoint has a 60-second cooldown for that pair. It must be seen again after the cooldown before departure can start another journey; leaving during the cooldown does not start a return recording later. Other pairs remain independent. The cooldown survives restarting the app during the same phone boot and resets after reboot. Waiting at the origin is excluded, while stops during the journey are included. Manual times run from the button presses, so time spent waiting after Start is included in that teaching journey.

Only GPS fixes reporting accuracy of 35 m or better are used. Gaps over 30 seconds and implausible jumps interrupt automatic trips and break the drawn path. The app does not infer missing roads. Missing GPS or endpoints too close together preserve the manual recording under Other recordings without learning a pair.

The foreground service monitors with high-accuracy requests every 5 seconds while waiting and every second during capture; actual delivery depends on Android and GPS reception. Monitoring consumes battery. It continues with the screen off, but Android force-stop, restart, permission revocation, or OEM restrictions can stop it. Reopen the app after a reboot or force-stop to resume enabled monitoring. Offline recording works; uncached map tiles need network. Notifications are required before recording or monitoring can start. If permission or the Journey recording channel is blocked, the app explains how to enable it. Dismissing the notification or blocking it during tracking stops tracking and preserves partial recordings. The notification shows monitoring, recording, or waiting for GPS.

For a real-drive check, teach or map a pair, make an outward and a return journey, and take a different road for another outward trip. Check that all paths are separate, directions are correct, and timings are plausible. This remains the user's road test; do not operate the phone while driving. Completed checks and their limits are in [verification](verification.md).
